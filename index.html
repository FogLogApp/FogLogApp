Privacy Policy

FogLog · Last updated: August 2026

No servers. No accounts. No tracking.

Overview

FogLog is a concussion recovery companion. Your privacy is central to how it works: all data you enter stays on your device. FogLog has no servers and no accounts, and never sends your data anywhere by itself. The only exception is when you deliberately export or share it yourself, which is described below.

What FogLog stores

FogLog stores the following data, exclusively on your device:

  • Your injury date and days since injury
  • Daily symptom ratings (headache, brain fog, fatigue, light sensitivity, sound sensitivity, neck pain, back pain, eye strain)
  • How you slept and your energy level, plus any note you write
  • Exercise completion history and any exercises you add yourself
  • App preferences (notification times, text size, bedtime target)

Nobody else can reach this data. It is not uploaded, and FogLog has no way to read it.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you choose to enable Apple Health integration, FogLog requests read-only access to your sleep analysis data. This is used solely to pre-fill your daily sleep log and display sleep information on your Today screen.

  • FogLog never writes data to Apple Health
  • Sleep data is read in real time and never stored separately
  • HealthKit access is entirely optional and can be revoked at any time via iOS Settings → Health → Data Access
  • No health data is transmitted off your device

Exporting and sharing your own data

FogLog lets you export everything it has stored, and lets you share a summary of your recent weeks to bring to an appointment. Both use the standard iOS share sheet, so you choose where the file goes.

  • Nothing is exported or shared unless you start it yourself
  • The file goes only to the destination you pick, such as Files, Mail, or Messages
  • Once it leaves FogLog, that copy is covered by the privacy policy of wherever you sent it, not by this one
  • Importing a file reads it from your device and overwrites your current data

Notifications

If you enable reminders, FogLog schedules local notifications on your device. These are processed entirely by iOS. No notification content is sent to any server.

Analytics and tracking

FogLog contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporting services, and no third-party tracking of any kind. Your usage of the app is not monitored.

Data storage and deletion

All data is stored in FogLog's own private area on your device, which iOS keeps separate from other apps. You can permanently delete everything using Settings → Reset all data, or by deleting the app from your device.

Children

FogLog is intended for people aged 16 and older, in line with GDPR Article 9 on the processing of health data, and does not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. Younger users should only use it with a parent or guardian.

Medical disclaimer

FogLog is not a medical device and does not provide diagnoses or treatment recommendations. Always follow the advice of your healthcare professionals. Use this app as a complement to, not a replacement for, professional care.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, you will see a notice when opening the app after an update. Continued use of the app after such notice constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact foglog.app@gmail.com
