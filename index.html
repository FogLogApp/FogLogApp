Privacy Policy

FogLog · Last updated: July 2026

No servers. No accounts. No tracking.

Overview

FogLog is a concussion recovery companion. Your privacy is central to how it works: all data you enter stays on your device. FogLog has no servers, no accounts, and sends nothing to the internet.

What FogLog stores

FogLog stores the following data, exclusively on your device:

  • Your injury date and days since injury
  • Daily symptom logs (headache, fatigue, mood, sleep, energy)
  • Exercise completion history
  • App preferences (notification times, text size, bedtime target)

None of this data is uploaded, shared, or accessible to anyone other than you.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you choose to enable Apple Health integration, FogLog requests read-only access to your sleep analysis data. This is used solely to pre-fill your daily sleep log and display sleep information on your Today screen.

  • FogLog never writes data to Apple Health
  • Sleep data is read in real time and never stored separately
  • HealthKit access is entirely optional and can be revoked at any time via iOS Settings → Health → Data Access
  • No health data is transmitted off your device

Notifications

If you enable reminders, FogLog schedules local notifications on your device. These are processed entirely by iOS. No notification content is sent to any server.

Analytics and tracking

FogLog contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporting services, and no third-party tracking of any kind. Your usage of the app is not monitored.

Data storage and deletion

All data is stored locally using iOS secure storage. You can permanently delete everything by using Settings → Reset all data, or by deleting the app from your device.

Children

FogLog is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children. The app is intended for individuals recovering from a concussion under the guidance of a healthcare professional.

Medical disclaimer

FogLog is not a medical device and does not provide diagnoses or treatment recommendations. Always follow the advice of your healthcare professionals. Use this app as a complement to, not a replacement for, professional care.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, you will see a notice when opening the app after an update. Continued use of the app after such notice constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
